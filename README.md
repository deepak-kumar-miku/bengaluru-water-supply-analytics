# Bengaluru Water Supply & Tanker Dependency Analytics

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI analytics project focused on understanding Bengaluru's water supply situation, regional water availability, tanker dependency, public complaints, and water quality.

The project uses data modelling, DAX measures, and interactive visualizations to explore water-related challenges across different zones.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. City Water Pulse
Provides an overview of the water supply situation through key performance indicators and summary visualizations.

### 2. Zone Reliability
- Compares water supply across zones.
- Analyses water demand versus supply.
- Examines tanker trips by zone.
- Highlights differences in supply gaps.

### 3. Tanker Dependency
- Analyses tanker trips across zones.
- Tracks monthly tanker activity.
- Compares tanker dependency percentages.
- Examines changes in tanker usage over time.

### 4. Complaints & Water Quality
- Analyses complaints by zone.
- Examines complaint resolution status.
- Tracks open and escalated complaints.
- Visualizes water quality records across zones.
